- Cares about: {persona_priorities}
- Pain points: {persona_pain_points}

TASK: Write a compelling email that provides value and drives action.

STRUCTURE:

Subject Line:
- 40-60 characters max
- Create curiosity or promise value
- Avoid spam triggers (FREE, ACT NOW, etc.)
- Examples: "The metric 87% of facilities miss", "Quick question about [pain point]"

Preview Text:
- 80-100 characters
- Complement the subject, don't repeat it
- Expand on the promise

Body:
1. Opening hook (1-2 sentences)
   - Reference their world/pain point
   - Don't start with "I" or company name

2. Value delivery (2-3 short paragraphs)
   - Share the insight/data/story
   - Make it immediately useful
   - Use bullet points for scanability

3. Bridge to CTA (1 sentence)
   - Connect the value to the next step

4. CTA
   - Single, clear action
   - Button text should be action-oriented
   - Example: "Get the Framework" not "Click Here"

5. Sign-off
   - Warm but professional
   - Use placeholder: [Your Name] or [Your Name, Title - Company]

REQUIREMENTS:
- Total length: 150-250 words
- Reading time: Under 2 minutes
- Mobile-friendly formatting
- One primary CTA only
- P.S. line optional (great for secondary offer)

AVOID:
- Multiple CTAs competing for attention
- Wall of text
- Starting with "Hi [Name]" (we'll personalize later)
- Generic closings like "Best regards"
