# ContentMultiplier Beta Testing Guide

Welcome to the ContentMultiplier beta! This guide will help you get started.

---

## What Is ContentMultiplier?

An AI tool that transforms your long-form content (webinars, podcasts, conference talks) into multiple pieces of written content (LinkedIn posts, blog posts, emails) while maintaining your brand voice.

**Key benefit**: Turns 1 hour of video into 10+ pieces of publication-ready content in 15 minutes.

---

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Register

1. Go to the app URL (provided separately)
2. Click "Register"
3. Enter your email and a password (8+ characters)
4. You're in!

### Step 2: Upload Your First Content

1. Click "Upload Content" or go to the Upload page
2. Choose a file:
   - **Video (MP4, MOV)**: Webinar recording, conference talk, training video
   - **Audio (MP3, M4A)**: Podcast episode, interview recording
   - **PDF**: Event transcript, whitepaper, article
   - **Text**: Paste transcript directly

3. Select your **target audience**:
   - **CEO (Executive)**: ROI-focused, high-level, time-constrained
   - **Director of Nursing**: Care-quality focused, clinical but accessible
   - **Marketing Director**: Brand and positioning focused, strategic

4. Choose **what to generate**:
   - LinkedIn posts (select 1-5 variations)
   - Blog post (comprehensive article)
   - Email sequence (coming soon)

5. **Optional - Add "Magic Words"**:
   - Brand names, acronyms, technical terms
   - Example: `SafelyYou, Q4, ROI, DON, CMS`
   - This helps transcription accuracy for domain-specific terminology

6. Click **"Upload & Process"**

### Step 3: Watch the Magic

You'll be taken to a status page where you can:
- See real-time progress through each step
- Watch your transcript appear as it's processed
- Read tips while waiting (we'll teach you about the pipeline!)
- Typical processing time: **10-15 minutes** for a 1-hour video

**Processing Steps**:
1. Transcription - Converting audio to text
2. Atomization - Extracting reusable content pieces
3. Drafting - Creating content with Claude (brand voice)
4. Editing - Optimizing for your target audience
5. Fact-checking - Verifying against source material
6. Scoring - Rating quality of each output

### Step 4: Review Results

When processing completes, you'll see:

**Generated Content**:
- Each piece of content grouped by type (LinkedIn, Blog)
- **Quality scores** (0-100) for each output
- Hover over scores to see breakdown:
  - Hook Quality
  - Brand Alignment
  - Clarity
  - Engagement Potential

**Actions**:
- Copy any content to clipboard with one click
- View different versions (draft, edited, final)
- See warnings if any claims need verification

**Extracted Atoms**:
- Reusable content pieces from your source
- Data points, insights, stories, problems, solutions
- Filter by type
- Use these to generate more content later!

### Step 5: Use Your Content Library

Every upload adds to your personal Content Library:

1. Go to **Library** page
2. Browse all atoms from all your uploads
3. Filter by type (data, insight, story, etc.)
4. Select atoms you want to use
5. Click **"Generate from Selected Atoms"**
6. Get new content without uploading anything!

## Pro Tips

### For Best Results

1. **Longer content = more atoms**: A 1-hour webinar yields more content than a 5-minute clip
2. **Use magic words**: Add your brand names and acronyms for accurate transcription
3. **Choose the right persona**: The persona affects vocabulary, length, and focus
4. **Check quality scores**: Higher scores typically mean less editing needed

### Understanding Quality Scores

| Score | Rating | What It Means |
|-------|--------|---------------|
| 85+ | Excellent | Near publication-ready |
| 70-84 | Good | Minor edits recommended |
| 50-69 | Fair | Needs some work |
| <50 | Needs Work | Significant editing required |

### Testing Brand Voice (New!)

Before processing, you can test how content will sound:
1. Go to **Settings** page
2. Click **Brand Voice Testing** tab
3. Paste sample text
4. Select a persona
5. Click **Preview Transformation**
6. See side-by-side before/after comparison

## FAQ

**Q: How long does processing take?**
A: Typically 10-15 minutes for 1 hour of content. Shorter content processes faster.

**Q: What file size limit?**
A: 500MB maximum. Most 1-hour videos are under 200MB.

**Q: Can I edit the prompts?**
A: Beta testers have read-only access. Let us know if you want prompt customization!

**Q: What happens to my uploaded files?**
A: Files are stored securely and only you can access them. We don't share or use your content.

**Q: Can I delete my content?**
A: Yes! Go to Library → select items → Delete. (Feature coming soon)

**Q: What if processing fails?**
A: You'll see a detailed error message with suggested actions. Try again or contact support.
